Your subscription is active. Three short steps and your whole team works without limits: as many landscapes and nodes as you need.

  1. Find your licence key

    We sent it to you by e-mail, from Polar, who handles the payment for us. It looks like ARCHIPEL-XXXX-XXXX-…

    No e-mail after a few minutes? Check your spam folder, or find the key in your account: sign in with the same e-mail address, no password needed.

    Open my account

  2. Activate it in Archipel

    Open Archipel and go to

    Settings›Licence

    Turn on Edit, paste the key and choose Activate. Then save: the key is kept with the workspace, so everyone who works in it is licensed too. One key is for one workspace.

    Download Archipel

  3. Manage or cancel, any time

    In your account you change your plan, update your card, download your invoices (with VAT) and cancel your subscription. No call, no notice period.

    After you cancel, Archipel keeps its licence until the end of the period you paid for. Then it is the free version again: one landscape and 50 nodes. Nothing you made is removed.
