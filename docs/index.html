1 · See

Know what you have, and how it is doing.

Every application, place and connection in one model, with owners, lifecycle, costs and the traffic that really flows. Archipel checks it for you.

  • A health score per landscape, built from checks: broken references, processes that miss an input, products going out of support, failed traffic.
  • What breaks without it? Take a node away and see every process that stops, before anyone decides.
  • A time line that plays the landscape month by month: what is being built, what is ending, how it looks after a project.
  • Portfolio facts: criticality, TIME (invest, tolerate, migrate, eliminate), costs per vendor and per year, end of support.
Health
Processes and capabilities

See how the business runs on it.

Draw a process across the systems it touches, and Archipel checks it against the landscape: who supplies each input, and what is missing.

  • Process flows with a lane per application, steps by hand and by system, decisions and events. Export as BPMN.
  • As-is and to-be in one flow: what ends is drawn dashed red, what is planned comes in on its date.
  • A capability map per landscape: what serves each capability, where there is overlap, and the gaps that matter.
  • Business objects and a CRUD matrix: where data comes from and where it goes.

2 · Decide

Decisions with the why, in one register.

Anyone can ask the architects a question. It becomes a numbered decision, weighed against your strategy and principles, decided, and kept with its reasons.

  • An architecture decision register (ADR-001 and on): asked, in work, proposed, decided, with who decided and when to look again.
  • Strategy that is used: goals, courses of action and principles; every option says which it follows, or why it knowingly deviates.
  • Software selection, step by step: requirements with MoSCoW, a longlist, knock-outs, a shortlist with the cost over five years, PoC's, advice and the choice.
  • The choice lands in the model: the product goes into your software list with its vendor and costs.

3 · Change

Know the impact before anyone says yes.

Propose to add, retire or replace something, and Archipel shows at once which processes break, which connections must move and which projects touch the same systems.

  • Change requests that the owners of what they touch assess, and the architects approve.
  • Projects on a time line: every change names its project, so the future landscape is planned, not guessed.
  • Reviews like code: changes to the model are reviewed on GitHub before they count, with the history of every line.

4 · Tell

Present the real thing, not a screenshot of it.

Slides in Archipel show the landscape, a process, a roadmap or a decision as it is today. Change the model and the presentation changes with it.

  • Living slides: the 3D landscape from a chosen spot and date, with notes pinned to nodes, a time jump from as-is to to-be.
  • A presenter view on your own screen with your notes, the next slide and the time.
  • Copies for an audience: one read-only file that leaves out what they need not see.

How it works

Your model, in your Git. Archipel only opens it.

There is no Archipel server with your landscapes. The model is a folder of readable files in a GitHub repository your organisation owns; Archipel, in the browser or on the desktop, reads and writes it.

Your repository Plain YAML files: landscapes, processes, decisions, change requests.
landscapes/platform/nodes/erp.yaml processes/order-to-cash.yaml decisions/which-ciam.yaml
⇄
Archipel In Chrome or Edge, or the app for Windows and macOS. It draws, checks and presents the model.
⇄
Your team Invited with one link. Each works in a draft; a review makes it count.
Never locked inReadable files in your own repository: leave whenever you like.
Nothing to hostNo database, no server to run. It keeps working if a licence server is gone.
