Enterprise architecture, without the platform

What you have, where you stand, where you build.

Archipel shows your applications, places and processes as islands in a sea, a landscape everyone in the room understands. With the decisions, change requests and projects that move it, and the files kept in your own Git repository.

Look around in an example Open Archipel in the browser

The example is an invented company, view only: nothing to sign in, nothing to install.

One shared picture instead of ten diagrams

Most landscapes live in drawings and spreadsheets that are out of date the day after. In Archipel the model is the picture: change it once and every view, report and presentation follows.

A landscape you read at a glance

Applications and places stand on islands like a model city; connections are pipes in the colour of what they carry. Move the time line to see what is being built and what is ending.

Processes, data and capabilities

Business processes as flows, the objects they create and use, and a capability map that shows where you are thin. All tied to the same applications.

Architecture decisions

Anyone asks a question; the architects weigh the options against the strategy, decide and say why. A register with numbers, reviews and a log you can print.

Change requests with their impact

Propose to add, retire or replace something and see at once which processes break and which connections must move. The owners assess it; a project carries it out.

Software selection, step by step

Requirements with MoSCoW, a longlist, knock-outs, a shortlist with the cost price, PoC's, advice and the choice, which goes straight into your software list.

Presentations from the live model

Slides that show the landscape, a roadmap or a decision as it is today, with speaker notes and a presenter view. No screenshots that go stale.

Up and running in a coffee break

No server to install, no project to start. A workspace is a folder of plain files.

Open it

In the browser, or as an app for Windows and macOS. Start with the example to see how it feels.

Draw or import your landscape

Add islands and applications by hand, or bring them in from ArchiMate, Structurizr or C4-PlantUML.

Invite your colleagues

Share the workspace through GitHub with an invite link. Changes are reviewed before they count, like code.

Your data stays yours

  • Everything is kept as readable YAML files in a folder or a Git repository you own.
  • There is no Archipel server with your landscapes: the app opens your files and that is it.
  • Git gives you the history, the reviews and the backups, with the tools you already have.
  • Leave whenever you like: the files are yours, in a format anyone can read.
# your repository workspace.yaml landscapes/ platform/ landscape.yaml nodes/webshop.yaml processes/order-to-cash.yaml decisions/which-ciam.yaml change-requests/one-customer-login.yaml settings/products.yaml
